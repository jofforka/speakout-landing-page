Privacy Policy

SpeakOut Mental Health Outreach

Last updated: September 25, 2026

SpeakOut Mental Health Outreach (“SpeakOut”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use our public website, programs, forms and related digital services.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, school or organization, program interests, volunteer or partnership details, and messages submitted through forms.

Our website may also collect limited technical information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, approximate location derived from IP address, referral source, and similar usage information through hosting, security, analytics or advertising technologies.

2. How we use information

  • Respond to enquiries, registrations, applications and partnership requests.
  • Operate school, outreach, academy, media and volunteer programs.
  • Improve website usability, security, accessibility and content.
  • Measure aggregate engagement and program reach.
  • Meet safeguarding, legal, administrative and record-keeping responsibilities.
  • Communicate important service or program updates where appropriate.

3. Mental-health and sensitive information

SpeakOut’s public website is primarily educational. Public website content is not intended to diagnose, treat or replace professional medical care. Where a separate service collects wellbeing or other sensitive information, that service may have an additional privacy notice explaining its specific data practices. MindCheck, for example, should be reviewed together with its own privacy notice.

4. Children and young people

SpeakOut works with schools and young people. We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant activity and to apply appropriate safeguarding and privacy protections. Some services may require involvement or consent from a parent, guardian, school or responsible adult depending on the participant’s age, the service and applicable requirements.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, security, preferences, analytics and, when enabled, advertising.

We may use Google AdSense and related Google services. When advertising is enabled, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and improve ads. Google’s advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites, subject to applicable consent and personalization rules.

Visitors can manage Google ad personalization through Google’s Ads Settings. Where required, we will use an appropriate consent mechanism before non-essential advertising or measurement technologies are activated.

Because SpeakOut includes youth and health-related content, we do not intend to use sensitive health information or known child-directed activity to personalize advertising. Advertising settings and page eligibility should be configured accordingly.

6. Third-party services and links

Our site may link to or use third-party services such as payment providers, social platforms, video platforms, learning providers, messaging services, analytics tools, form services and cloud infrastructure. Those services operate under their own privacy terms.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers, program partners or authorities where reasonably necessary to operate a service, protect users, comply with law, prevent abuse, or respond to safeguarding concerns.

8. Data retention and security

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legitimate administrative, safeguarding, contractual or legal needs. We use reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices

Depending on applicable law and the context, you may be able to request access, correction or deletion of personal information you provided, withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing, or ask questions about our data practices.

10. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as our services, partners or legal obligations change. The “Last updated” date above will show the latest revision.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact SpeakOut Mental Health Outreach at speaaakout@gmail.com.

Important: SpeakOut provides educational and early-support information. It is not a substitute for professional medical care, diagnosis or emergency services.
