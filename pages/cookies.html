Cookie Policy

SpeakOut Mental Health Outreach

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Cookies and similar technologies are small pieces of information that websites and service providers can store or read on a device. SpeakOut may use them to operate and secure the site, remember preferences, understand aggregate usage and, when enabled, support advertising.

Types of technologies we may use

  • Essential: required for security, navigation, authentication or core functionality.
  • Preference: remember choices made by a visitor.
  • Analytics: help us understand how public pages are used and improve performance.
  • Advertising: when enabled, help third-party advertising providers serve, limit, measure or personalize ads in accordance with consent and policy requirements.

Google advertising

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery and measurement. Google advertising settings and legally required consent choices will be respected.

Consent

Where consent is legally required for non-essential technologies, SpeakOut will use an appropriate consent mechanism. For EEA, UK and Swiss traffic served through Google publisher products, the advertising implementation should use a Google-certified consent management platform where required.

Managing cookies

You can usually manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Some site functions may not work correctly if essential technologies are blocked. Advertising preferences for Google services can also be managed through Google’s Ads Settings.

Contact

Questions about cookies or privacy can be sent to speaaakout@gmail.com.
